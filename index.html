From recorder to insight

The flight data chain.

Skye Data carries your data the whole way, from opaque binary to a regulator-ready safety story.

Source

The aircraft writes a diary, hundreds of parameters, many times a second.

Capture

The recorder packs it into ARINC 717 frames. Crash-survivable, unreadable.

Decode

Your frame definition is the key. Bits become engineering values.

Values

A clean time-series in proper units, ready to analyse.

Report

Exceedances and events surfaced. Regulator-ready PDF plus an interactive review.
